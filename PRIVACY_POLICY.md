# Flikk Play — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 27 November 2026_

Flikk Play ("the app") is a video and music player for Android, made in India. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## Summary

- Flikk Play does **not** collect, store or sell any personal information.
- The app has **no account, no login, no ads and no analytics/tracking**.
- Your videos, music and settings stay **on your phone**.

## Permissions the app uses and why

| Permission | Why it is needed |
|---|---|
| Videos / Music (media access) | To find and play the video and audio files on your phone. |
| Internet & network state | Only when **you** choose to: play a video link (URL), open a PC/NAS folder on your Wi-Fi, or cast to a TV. |
| Notifications & foreground service | To show playback controls while music or video plays in the background. |
| Biometric (fingerprint) | Only to unlock the Private folder, if you turn it on. Your fingerprint is checked by Android; the app never sees it. |

## Data stored on your device

The app saves a few things **only on your phone** so it works the way you expect: resume positions, playlists, favourites, bookmarks, settings, the Private-folder list and a scrambled (hashed) form of your Private-folder PIN. This data is excluded from cloud backup and is removed when you uninstall the app.

## Network features

- **PC / NAS (SMB):** connects only to the computer or drive address you enter, on your network. Any password you type is stored only on your phone.
- **Cast to TV:** uses Google Cast to send the video you choose to a TV on the same Wi-Fi. While casting, the app serves that one video from your phone over your local network only.
- **Open URL:** streams only the link you enter.

The app does not send your files or any information about you to the developer or any other server.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
